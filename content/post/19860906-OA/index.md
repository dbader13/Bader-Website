Vigil Honor, Order of the Arrow

Boy Scouts of America

This Certifies that **David Bader** is award the Vigil Honor

In recognition of exceptional service, personal effort, and unselfish interest in the welfare of others. He has made distinguished contributions above and beyond his immediate responsibilities in the Order of the Arrow as a member of Witauchsoman Lodge in the Minsi Trails Council. He was recorded as a Vigil member on September 6, 1986.  His Indian name is Notaski interpreted as He Who Thinks.

G. Allen Mossman
For Vigil Honor
