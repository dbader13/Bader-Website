On Tuesday, September 8th, the ECE Department held its annual Fall Awards Ceremony and Reception.  Awards were presented to students, staff and faculty by ECE Chair Sennur Ulukus. The following awards were presented:

* **The George Corcoran Memorial Award for Faculty**, presented annually to a young faculty member who has shown exemplary contributions to teaching and educations leadership: Professor Zhambyl Shaikhanov

* **Jimmy H.C. Lin Award for Innovation and Invention** awarded annually to a faculty member who has transformed their ideas into innovations through invention and technology commercialization: Professor Cheng Gong for his patent, “Energy Efficient Ferroelectric Device and Method for Making the Same”

* **Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering Staff Service Award** in recognition of a staff member for outstanding service to faculty, staff and students: Paris Christian, Coordinator, Chair’s Office

* **The David Bader Graduate Student Service Award** in recognition of exceptional service to the department: Irem Didin

* **George Corcoran Memorial Award for a Graduate Student** presented to a graduate teaching assistant in recognition of excellence in teaching: Chidiebere Udenze

* **The Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering Distinguished Dissertation Award**, in recognition of recent doctoral recipients who have already made unusually significant and original contributions to their fields.  This year’s recipients are:
    * Sagnik Bhattacharya, advised by Professor Prakash Narayan  
*Dissertation: Global computation from local information: classical and quantum*
    * Shanchuan Liang, advised by Professor Cheng Gong  
*Dissertation: Magnetoelectric Effects in Two-Dimensional Multiferroic Heterostructures*
    * Sahan Liyanaarachchi, advised by Professor Sennur Ulukus  
*Dissertation: Freshness-Based Remote Estimation and Control*
    * Sydney Overton, advised by Professor Reza Ghodssi  
*Dissertation: An Ingestible Capsule for Gastrointestinal Serotonin Sensing to Investigate the Gut-Brain Axis*
    * Supratik Sarkar, advised by Professor Mohammad Hafezi  
*Dissertation: Intersection of many-body physics and photonics: 2D semiconductors and PICs*
